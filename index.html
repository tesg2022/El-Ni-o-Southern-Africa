Preparedness

1. Climate

Monitor rainfall deficits, heat anomalies and evolving ENSO signals at weekly to monthly timescales.

Water

2. Water security

Combine precipitation, evapotranspiration, runoff, soil moisture and storage indicators to anticipate supply stress.

Groundwater

3. Recharge protection

Flag areas where rainfall deficits, high ET, declining soil moisture and runoff changes may reduce recharge potential.

Agriculture

Overlay cropland with rainfall, soil moisture and vegetation anomalies to identify production-risk hotspots and guide early action.

Ecosystems

Use NDVI/vegetation condition, water availability and land-cover information to detect ecosystem stress and restoration priorities.

Groundwater recharge

For research-grade assessment, integrate JULES soil moisture and subsurface runoff with observed climate indicators rather than treating a simple proxy as measured recharge.
